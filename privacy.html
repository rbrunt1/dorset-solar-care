Cookies

This site sets no tracking or advertising cookies. We record how you found us using your browser's sessionStorage, which is first-party, holds no identifier, and is deleted when you close the tab. Visitor numbers come from our host's server logs rather than from tracking scripts in your browser. That is why you are not being shown a cookie banner — there is nothing to consent to.

Who we share it with

  • Netlify — hosting and storage of form submissions
  • Resend — sends us the notification email when you get in touch, and sends you an automatic confirmation that we’ve received it. That confirmation is a one-off reply to your enquiry — we don’t add you to a mailing list and won’t email you about anything else unless you ask us to.
  • GoCardless — Direct Debit mandates and collection
  • Namecheap Private Email — our email inbox
  • Google Search Console — aggregate search statistics, no personal data

We do not sell your data, and we do not pass it to marketing lists.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that don't become customers: 12 months. Customer records and visit history: for the life of your plan and 6 years afterwards, which is how long we must keep records for tax and liability purposes.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, to delete it, to restrict or object to how we use it, or to receive it in a portable format. Email hello@solarmot.co.uk and we will respond within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we've handled your data you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.
