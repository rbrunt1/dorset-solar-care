About & trust

Who's actually coming up your ladder

Solar panel maintenance means working near live electrical systems. Here's what we do to make sure it's done safely and properly.

The company

A registered UK Ltd company, built for the long term

SolarMOT is a private limited company registered in England & Wales. We built the business around ongoing subscriptions rather than one-off jobs because we think solar maintenance works better as a relationship — regular visits, a consistent point of contact, and a team that gets to know your system over time.

We hold public liability insurance appropriate to working at height and around electrical installations.

At a glance

  • Based in and launched from Dorset
  • Maintenance, cleaning & inspection specialists
Qualifications

Trained for the electrical side of solar, specifically

Anyone on our team who works near live PV or inverter circuitry holds relevant City & Guilds qualifications for that work — not just general cleaning experience.

What we don't do

SolarMOT is a maintenance, cleaning and inspection business — we are not an MCS-certified installer and we do not design, sell or install new solar PV systems. If you're looking to have panels installed for the first time, we can point you toward MCS-certified installers in the area, but our subscriptions are for systems you already have.
