Commercial

Commercial & larger systems: £4–£15 per kWp/year

Priced per kWp of installed capacity and service tier, billed monthly. The right price depends on your system size, panel accessibility, and how often you want us on site — so commercial plans start with a short quote rather than instant checkout.

Request a commercial quote

How it's priced

£4–£15 per kWp per year, billed monthly, based on system size (kWp) and the service level you choose — from a basic annual clean to a full quarterly maintenance contract.

Who it's for

Commercial rooftop and ground-mount arrays, farms, warehouses, schools and any business running solar at scale across Dorset.

What you get

A tailored maintenance schedule, inspection reporting, and a named point of contact — agreed after your quote, before any Direct Debit is set up.
