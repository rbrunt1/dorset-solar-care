Commercial

Larger systems: £4–£15 per kWp per year

Priced on installed capacity and service level, billed monthly, with a minimum contract of £600 a year. What you pay depends on system size, panel accessibility and how often you want us on site — so commercial plans start with a short quote rather than instant checkout.

Request a commercial quote

How it's priced

£4–£15 per kWp per year, billed monthly, based on installed capacity and the service level you pick — from a basic annual MOT to a full quarterly maintenance contract. The lower end of that range applies to larger arrays, where the work per kWp drops. Minimum contract £600 a year; under roughly 20 kWp you're usually better off on one of the residential plans above.

Who it's for

Commercial rooftop and ground-mount arrays, farms, warehouses, schools, and any business running solar at scale within our coverage area.

What you get

A tailored maintenance schedule, formal inspection reporting for your records, and a named point of contact — all agreed after the quote, before any Direct Debit is set up.
