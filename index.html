Why it matters

Panels are a 25-year investment. Most get checked once — at install.

Dust, pollen, bird mess, moss and coastal salt air quietly rob you of output every day they're left alone. And small faults left unnoticed have a habit of becoming expensive ones.

Protect the asset

A solar array is a serious upfront spend. Routine cleaning and inspection protects it and helps it deliver the payback period you actually planned for.

Keep the output up

Soiled or partially shaded panels can lose a meaningful share of their generating capacity. Regular cleaning keeps the system producing what it was designed to.

Catch faults early

Loose connections, degraded seals and early-stage inverter issues cost far less to fix when spotted on a routine visit than after a breakdown.

Plain English

"MOT" is our shorthand, not a legal test

There's no government-mandated inspection for solar panels — we borrow the term because everyone already understands what an MOT is: a thorough, documented, once-a-year-minimum check by a qualified person, with a clear pass or fail.

That's exactly what we do for your array. What we're not is an MCS-certified installer — we don't fit new systems, we look after the one you've got.

Common questions

Solar panel maintenance, answered

How often should solar panels be cleaned in the UK?

Most UK domestic arrays need cleaning once or twice a year. Roofs near the coast, under trees, or close to farmland or busy roads soil faster and benefit from twice-yearly cleaning; a sheltered inland roof is usually fine annually. We don't sell quarterly cleaning, because for the large majority of homes it isn't necessary.

Does cleaning solar panels actually make a difference to output?

Yes, though how much depends entirely on how dirty they are. Rain rinses off light dust, but it does not shift bird droppings, moss, lichen or the greasy film that builds up near roads and coastlines. Localised soiling matters more than it looks: shading even part of one panel can drag down a whole string, so a small amount of dirt in the wrong place costs more than an even layer of dust.

What is a Solar MOT?

It's our name for a documented annual check of your solar system: a full panel clean, plus an inspection of mounting and fixings, cabling and connectors, isolators, seals and weatherproofing, and the inverter and its error codes — finished with a written report and an output reading. "MOT" is used descriptively. It is not a statutory test and has no legal standing.

Do you install solar panels?

No. SolarMOT is a maintenance company only — we are not an MCS-certified installer and we don't sell or fit systems. We maintain arrays that somebody else installed, which is why every new customer starts with a baseline survey of a system we've not seen before.

What happens if you find a fault?

Minor repairs — fixings, seals, connectors and cable management — are included on the Standard and Premium plans and charged separately on Essential. Anything larger is quoted before any work starts, with a 20% discount on Premium. We never carry out chargeable work without telling you first.
