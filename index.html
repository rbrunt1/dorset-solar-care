Why maintenance matters

Panels are a 25-year investment. Treat them like one.

Dust, pollen, bird mess, moss and salt air (hello, Dorset coastline) quietly rob you of output every single day they're left unchecked — and small faults left unnoticed can become expensive ones.

Protect your investment

A solar array is a significant upfront spend. Regular cleaning and inspection protects that asset and helps it deliver the payback you planned for.

Maintain output & efficiency

Dirty or shaded panels can lose a meaningful chunk of their generating capacity. Routine cleaning keeps your system producing what it was designed to.

Catch faults early

Loose connections, degraded seals and early-stage inverter issues are far cheaper to fix when caught during a routine visit than after a breakdown.

The subscription difference

One-off cleans get forgotten. A subscription doesn't.

Most local cleaners — and even window-cleaning firms that have added solar to their round — sell a single visit or lock you into a long fixed term. Life gets busy, the booking never gets made again, and your panels quietly lose performance.

We built Dorset Solar Care around an ongoing relationship instead: scheduled visits happen automatically, and you can leave anytime on our Essential plan. It's the same shift Starks Solar in Ringwood pioneered locally — we've taken it further with more flexibility and no lock-in on the entry tier.
